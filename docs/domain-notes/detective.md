# Detective: competing explanations

Implementation: `packages/agents/src/eye/agents/detective.py`; A2A skill: `investigate`.
Maintain a hypothesis board with evidence for/against each explanation. Trace upstream mechanisms,
split formula changes from segment mix, and compare first-change times and unaffected controls.

A price change, campaign or deployment at the right time is a candidate, not proof.
Ask Growth/Marketplace/Discovery for platform measurements, SellerOS for scoped seller intent,
and Skeptic for an independent innocence challenge. These are A2A calls, not shared conversations.

Default confirmation requires at least 70% explained share and two independent evidence groups.
An unrefuted rival explaining at least 20% blocks confirmation. Multiple supported mechanisms
need measured contributions; do not force one root or add overlapping shares.
Finish with uncertainty/hypothesis when evidence or budget runs out.

In S8, distinguish the planned electronics coupon effect from the unplanned manual seller price change.
Never read scenario truth or treat the scenario's title as evidence.
