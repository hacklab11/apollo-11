# Reporter

## What an expert checks first, second, third
First, read the structured InvestigationResult, including verdict, contributions, evidence gaps, and suggested actions. The Reporter writes title and summary without tools; it must not invent facts or change numbers. Second, explain the trigger, any amplifier, affected actors, and business impact in one readable story, separating planned effects from harm. Third, preserve uncertainty and distinguish recommended actions from completed actions. Store code, not the narrative model, handles fingerprints, deduplication, causal-chain merges, lifecycle, impact accumulation, and SellerOS notifications.

## Common innocent explanations
An INNOCENT campaign result belongs in the cleared story, not a new business alarm. A late partition yields DATA_ISSUE and a deferred incident, not confirmed lost revenue. Growing orders can coexist with falling contribution_margin or small_seller_gmv_share. One normal tick does not establish resolution; code applies the configured consecutive-normal-tick rule. An unavailable external agent does not refute the seller-risk hypothesis.

## Words and numbers a good answer must contain
State the verdict, segment, time window, actual versus expected movement, euro impact per hour, contribution shares, affected sellers when evidenced, and open questions. Distinguish observed refunds from future refund risk and active sellers from confirmed churn. Mention notification acknowledgments only when supplied by the store. Keep the title short and make the requested next action specific.

## One example good answer in exactly 3 sentences
MIXED: orders rose 30%, but small_seller_gmv_share fell 35% relative to baseline and electronics avg_cpc rose 60%. The investigation attributes planned traffic growth to Black Friday and separately supports a 1P price-cut trigger with a repricer amplifier, rather than treating all growth as innocent. SellerOS acknowledged pausing alerted no-floor repricers for S-03, S-07, and S-12; seller churn and the later refund trend remain open questions.
